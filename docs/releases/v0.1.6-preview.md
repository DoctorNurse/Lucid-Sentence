## What's new

- **Documents open in the apps again.** In 0.1.5-preview, the Android and desktop apps could get stuck on "Opening…" when you opened a `.docx` or started a new document. The app's security settings kept its own built-in document engine from loading. Fixed, and the engine still runs entirely on your device, offline from the first launch.
- **No more endless "Opening…".** While a document opens, Lucid Sentence shows what it's doing. If something goes wrong, it says what happened and offers **Retry**.

Everything from 0.1.5-preview is here too: open and save `.docx` (pictures included), tables, headers and footers, print and PDF, spell check in 5 languages, the opt-in offline AI assistant, and smaller per-CPU Android downloads.

On an older 32-bit (armv7) Android phone still on 0.1.3-preview or earlier, download `Lucid-Sentence-Android-armv7.apk` once yourself; updates work after that.

This is still a preview: not every ribbon command works with Word documents yet, and the apps have had little testing on real devices.
