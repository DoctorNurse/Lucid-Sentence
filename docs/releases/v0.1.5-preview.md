## What's new

- **Pictures in your documents show up.** In 0.1.4-preview, pictures already inside a `.docx` you opened showed as empty frames. They now appear as they should. Pictures you insert worked before and still do. Saving never lost the pictures.

Everything from 0.1.4-preview is here too: open and save `.docx`, tables, pictures, headers and footers, print and PDF, spell check in 5 languages, the opt-in offline AI assistant, and smaller per-CPU Android downloads.

On an older 32-bit (armv7) Android phone still on 0.1.3-preview or earlier, download `Lucid-Sentence-Android-armv7.apk` once yourself; updates work after that.

This is still a preview: not every ribbon command works with Word documents yet, and the apps have had little testing on real devices.
