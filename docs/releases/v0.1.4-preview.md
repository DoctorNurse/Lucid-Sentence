## What's new in 0.1.4-preview

**Lucid Sentence now opens and saves real Word documents.** The ONLYOFFICE document engine runs inside the app, on your device, with no server and no account.

- **Open and save `.docx`:** File › Open, Save, and Save As use your system's file dialogs (or the browser's, with a download fallback). Files saved by Microsoft Word open with their styles, lists, tables, pictures, sections, and comments.
- **Tables:** Insert › Table, then the Table Design and Table Layout tabs: insert and delete rows and columns, merge and split cells.
- **Pictures:** Insert › Pictures from your device; the Picture Format tab appears when one is selected.
- **Headers and footers:** Insert › Header, Footer, and Page Number, with the Header & Footer tab.
- **Print and PDF:** Ctrl+P prints the laid-out pages, and File › Export › PDF saves a PDF with its fonts embedded. In the desktop and Android apps, Print saves a PDF for you to print.
- **Spell check in 5 languages:** English (US and UK), Spanish, French, and German, all on the device. Review › Spelling (F7) and the right-click menu offer suggestions.
- **Offline AI assistant (opt-in):** Review › Assistant can rewrite, fix grammar, summarize, shorten, and expand text, and Tell Lucid turns a plain request into a command. It is off until you turn it on and download a model (Qwen3.5, 0.5 to 1.3 GB, once). Everything runs on your device: nothing you write leaves it. For now the assistant works on notes pages; Word documents in the engine come next.
- **Smaller Android downloads:** one APK per kind of processor. `Lucid-Sentence-Android.apk` is for nearly all phones and tablets (64-bit ARM).

**Older 32-bit Android phones (armv7):** the in-app update offers the 64-bit APK, which won't install on these phones. Download `Lucid-Sentence-Android-armv7.apk` from this page once; later updates then pick the right file.

**Still a preview.** Not every ribbon command works with Word documents yet, and these builds have had little testing on real phones, tablets, and computers. Please [report problems](https://github.com/DoctorNurse/Lucid-Sentence/issues/new).
