## What's new

- **"Made with Lucid Sentence" on exported files.** Saved `.docx` files and exported PDFs get a slim line at the top of page 1 with a link to Lucid Sentence:
  - **PDF:** it sits above the page margin and never covers your text. It doesn't print, readers can hide it in their viewer, and a short note about it can be closed.
  - **`.docx`:** it goes in the first-page header, so it's one line to delete, and your body text isn't changed.
  - Turn it off under **File > Options > Saving and exporting**.

Everything from 0.1.6-preview is here too: documents open in the apps (with progress and Retry if something goes wrong), open and save `.docx` with pictures, tables, headers and footers, print and PDF, spell check in 5 languages, the opt-in offline AI assistant, and per-CPU Android downloads.

On an older 32-bit (armv7) Android phone still on 0.1.3-preview or earlier, download `Lucid-Sentence-Android-armv7.apk` once yourself; updates work after that.

This is still a preview: not every ribbon command works with Word documents yet, and the apps have had little testing on real devices.
